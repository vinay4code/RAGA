Enterprise Billing and Subscription Policies

Version: v3.1 | Last Updated: 2024-04-01 | Department: Finance & Commercial Operations

1. Subscription Tiers

The enterprise platform offers three commercial plans:

  • Starter Tier: $150 per month, includes up to 5 user seats and 50,000 monthly API calls.
  • Professional Tier: $500 per month, includes up to 25 user seats and 250,000 monthly API calls.
  • Enterprise Tier: Custom annual contract, includes unlimited user seats, dedicated support, and customized API throughput.

2. Overage and Additional Usage Fees

If an enterprise customer exceeds their monthly allocated API limit, an overage charge of $0.05 per 1,000 additional API requests is applied directly to the month-end invoice. Storage beyond the 500GB provisioned quota is billed at $0.02 per gigabyte per month.

3. Billing Cycles and Invoicing

All subscription charges are billed on the 1st day of each calendar month. Invoices are payable net-30 days from date of issuance. Late payments exceeding 45 days incur a 1.5% monthly late finance fee.

4. Cancellation and Refund Policy

Subscription cancellations require a 30 days written notice submitted to billing@enterprise.com. Prepaid annual fees are non-refundable after the initial 14-day evaluation window.
